gdrive-ignore

Choose what Google Drive syncs, with .gitignore-style rules

Checking Google Drive…

These rules apply to every folder that has Apply global rules on. They are saved in , so you can also edit them in any text editor.

How it works

Google Drive for Desktop has no way to exclude files. gdrive-ignore keeps your folder in two-way sync with a Drive copy inside a folder that Drive syncs, and leaves out everything your rules ignore.

  • Add, edit, rename or delete files in your folder: the changes reach Google Drive and your other computers.
  • Changes made online or on another computer come back into your folder, exactly like normal Google Drive.
  • Files deleted in Google Drive are moved to the Windows Recycle Bin here, never deleted permanently.
  • Deleting a file by hand in the Drive copy never deletes it from your folder: gdrive-ignore puts it back.
  • If it cannot tell where a deletion came from, it changes nothing and asks you.
  • Ignored files never cross in either direction, and adding a rule never deletes anything.
  • Files are hardlinked: the Drive copy takes no extra disk space.

Add folder

Two-way, like normal Google Drive: changes and deletions in your folder reach Drive; changes and deletions made online or on another computer reach your folder (deleted files go to the Recycle Bin). Work in your folder, not in the Drive copy.

Ignore rules

One pattern per line, like .gitignore: node_modules/, *.log, /build, !keep.log. .driveignore files inside your folder are applied too. Ignored files never sync in either direction.

Stop syncing this folder?

Syncing stops. Nothing is deleted: your folder and the Drive copy both keep their files.
